# Reviewer selection protocol

SLAS requests preferred reviewers and permits requests to exclude conflicted reviewers. Final names must be approved by the author after a conflict check.

## Preferred-reviewer criteria

Select three to five researchers who collectively cover:

1. laboratory automation integration and device interfaces;
2. connected-laboratory or self-driving-laboratory architecture;
3. scheduling, execution monitoring, or recovery;
4. empirical software engineering, CSCW, or technical-community methods.

A preferred reviewer should have no recent collaboration with the author, no shared current institution, no prior access to the manuscript, and no financial or competitive conflict. Use institutional email addresses and current affiliations verified from official pages.

## Non-preferred reviewers

Exclude anyone with a direct commercial stake in a product evaluated in the manuscript, anyone who participated materially in the coded discussions and could reasonably be perceived as reviewing their own evidence, and anyone with a recent collaboration or active application relationship with the author.

## Submission record

For each proposed reviewer, retain name, degree, affiliation, institutional email, subject-matter rationale, conflict-check date, and the source used to verify current employment.
