Subject: Editorial inquiry — Resource manuscript on operational evidence in laboratory automation

Dear Patterns Editors,

I am writing to ask whether the enclosed manuscript concept would be suitable as a Resource article in Patterns.

The Lab Automation Observatory is a versioned, machine-readable research resource built from public practitioner evidence about laboratory automation. It contains a derived 55-discussion register, 45 analytical episodes, field-level metric inputs, source and claim audit ledgers, robustness analyses, JSON Schemas, executable code, regression tests, and governed records for troubleshooting questions, resolved knowledge, device interfaces, and run events. The release is designed for independent recoding, cross-community evidence studies, laboratory knowledge stewardship, prospective operational instrumentation, and AI-method evaluation.

The associated manuscript describes the resource architecture, construction, validation, empirical demonstration, limitations, and reuse agenda. It does not present the forum as an incident census and does not support vendor comparisons. Its principal prospective mechanism links execution observability with recovery after irreversible physical actions.

The current release is public and fully reproducible. Before formal submission, I would deposit the exact package in a persistent archive and add the DOI. I would value the editors’ view on whether the combination of reusable data structures, software, governance, and empirical demonstration meets the journal’s Resource criteria, or whether broader external adoption should be demonstrated first.

Sincerely,
