## Digital Discovery

Official author guidance: https://www.rsc.org/journals-books-databases/author-and-reviewer-hub/authors-information/insights/2023/july/open-data-in-digital-discovery/

- Selected type: Full Paper.
- Code and data must be available to referees during peer review.
- Custom code and associated data must be placed in persistent repositories, with DOI information supplied for publication.
- A Data Availability Statement is required.
- The journal can assign a dedicated data reviewer to run the code and assess reproducibility and reuse.
- Manuscript: `paper/main_digital_discovery.tex`.

## Patterns

Official journal description: https://info.cell.com/patterns-registration

- Patterns publishes transformative data-science research across disciplines and promotes research outputs that support sharing, collaboration, and practical solutions.
- Cell Press expects relevant data and code to be accessible at submission.
- The present package is constructed as a Resource manuscript and remains conditional on editorial confirmation of article-type fit.
- A persistent archive DOI and evidence of reuse or adoption should exist before formal submission.
- Manuscript: `paper/main_patterns_resource.tex`.
- First contact: `paper/venues/patterns/editorial_inquiry.md`.

## CSCW Rolling 2027 and beyond

Official call: https://cscw.acm.org/rolling.html

- Submissions are evaluated on a rolling basis with no fixed deadline.
- Papers must make a social, cooperative, or collaborative computing contribution and be substantially contextualized in CSCW theory.
- Current tracks are qualitative and quantitative; this manuscript targets the qualitative track.
- Anonymous review applies to the manuscript and all supplementary files.
- LaTeX submissions must use the ACM `acmsmall` format and remain TAPS compliant.
- Papers below 5,000 words or above 12,000 words receive additional scrutiny.
- The cover letter must include a scope statement and submission-history statement.
- No overlapping archival work can remain under review elsewhere.
- Manuscript: `paper/main_cscw.tex`.

## Journal of Open Source Software

Official submission requirements: https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html

Official paper format: https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/paper.html

- The repository must have more than six months of public development with activity distributed across that period.
- The software must show demonstrated research impact and credible utility.
- Feature completeness, installation, documentation, tests, community contribution paths, and maintainable extension are required.
- The paper uses Markdown with YAML metadata and should contain 750--1750 words.
- The paper must focus on the research software and its applications, not present a new empirical study as the principal contribution.
- Current status: future only.
- Draft: `paper/venues/joss/paper.md`.
